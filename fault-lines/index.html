Book Two

Love is not only found. It is lived.

Michael, Juan, and Christopher have chosen a future together. Fault Lines begins after that choice becomes a home, a rhythm, and a life they share every day.

The next chapter follows what happens when love has to survive beyond the beginning—through pressure, old wounds, difficult conversations, changing expectations, and the ordinary work of continuing to choose one another.

If Book One was about discovering a love none of them expected, Book Two is about protecting the life they decided to build.

Inside Fault Lines

Book Two moves beyond the beginning of the relationship and into the harder, quieter work of protecting the life Michael, Juan, and Christopher chose together.
