A Throuple Love Story

What if your forever isn’t one person—but two?

Michael, Juan, and Christopher were never supposed to become a family. Yet somewhere between Navy ships, San Diego nights, difficult truths, and the quiet rituals of home, three lives begin to move as one.

Tres Amigos, Una Vida is an emotional gay and LGBTQ+ romance inspired by the lived truth of a real-life throuple. It explores vulnerability, jealousy, devotion, chosen family, and the courage required to be fully seen.

Behind the Book

Love does not need permission to become a life.

This story was born from the belief that love is limitless and layered—not perfect, not effortless, but deeply worth choosing.

It is about what happens when three men stop treating their connection like an exception and begin building a home where no one has to shrink to belong.

“Love is expansive, evolving, and yours to define.”
