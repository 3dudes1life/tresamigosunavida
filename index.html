A Throuple Love Story

What if your forever isn’t one person—but two?

Michael, Juan, and Christopher were never supposed to become a family. Yet somewhere between Navy ships, San Diego nights, hard truths, and the quiet rituals of home, three lives begin to move as one.

Tres Amigos, Una Vida is an LGBTQ+ romance inspired by the emotional truth of a real-life throuple. It follows three men as they confront vulnerability, jealousy, devotion, chosen family, and the terrifying beauty of being fully seen.

Three men. Three histories. One life they choose together.

Michael Rawlins

The steady Oklahoma cowboy carrying old expectations, quiet strength, and a heart far more vulnerable than he lets the world see.

Juan Delgado

The magnetic Florida-born DJ whose humor, warmth, and fearless love become the bridge holding three very different men together.

Christopher Lancaster

The composed Bay Area visionary who has built a successful life—but must learn that belonging cannot be organized, only trusted.

A romance about more than falling in love

This is a story about staying. About the daily work behind unconventional love. About choosing honesty when silence would be easier, and building a home where no one has to shrink to be accepted.
