A Throuple Love Story

What if your forever isn’t one person—but two?

Michael, Juan, and Christopher were never supposed to become a family. Yet somewhere between Navy ships, San Diego nights, difficult truths, and the quiet rituals of home, three lives begin to move as one.

Tres Amigos, Una Vida is an emotional gay and LGBTQ+ romance inspired by the lived truth of a real-life throuple. It explores vulnerability, jealousy, devotion, chosen family, and the courage required to be fully seen.

Meet the Trio

Michael Rawlins — The Cowboy

Quiet strength. Oklahoma roots. A steady presence carrying a heart far more vulnerable than he lets the world see.

Juan Delgado — The Artist

Ink, music, warmth, and fearless love. The magnetic bridge holding three very different men together.

Christopher Lancaster — The Visionary

A sharp mind behind gentle eyes, learning that belonging cannot be organized. It can only be trusted.

Behind the Book

This story was born from the belief that love is limitless and layered. It is not about perfection. It is about connection—and what happens when three men choose to build a life in a world that keeps asking them to explain it.

“Love is expansive, evolving, and yours to define.”

About D.C.W.

D.C.W. is Daniel, Caleb, and Will.

As a real-life throuple, they have built a home, a creative life, businesses, traditions, and a relationship together. Their experience gives the novel its emotional foundation: private humor, difficult conversations, fierce loyalty, and everyday tenderness.
