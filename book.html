The Novel

Tres Amigos, Una Vida

An intimate LGBTQ+ romance about three men discovering that love does not become less real when it expands.

Three hearts rewrite forever

An emotionally guarded Navy mechanic from Oklahoma. A tattooed culinary specialist from Florida. A sharp, self-contained tech professional from San Francisco.

Their paths cross through chance, timing, distance, and desire. What begins with friendship and attraction slowly grows into something none of them were taught to imagine: a committed life built by three men who refuse to treat one another as temporary.

Across deployments, crowded dance floors, family tension, new cities, private fears, and shared mornings, Michael, Juan, and Christopher learn that love is not sustained by chemistry alone. It survives through communication, forgiveness, vulnerability, and the decision to keep showing up.
