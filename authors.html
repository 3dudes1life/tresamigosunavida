About the Authors

D.C.W.

Daniel, Caleb, and Will write from inside the kind of love their fiction asks readers to believe is possible.

One pen name. Three perspectives.

Three lives behind one story

D.C.W. is the shared author identity of Daniel, Caleb, and Will—a real-life throuple who have spent years building a home, a creative life, and a relationship together.

Their experience does not make Tres Amigos, Una Vida autobiography. It gives the novel its emotional foundation: private humor, difficult conversations, fierce loyalty, and daily tenderness.

Through 3Dudes1Life Creative, they tell stories centered on LGBTQ+ lives, chosen family, and the belief that love does not need to resemble tradition to deserve permanence.
