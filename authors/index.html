About the Authors

D.C.W.

Daniel, Caleb, and Will are three authors writing under one shared name—and a real-life throuple bringing lived emotional truth to fictional LGBTQ+ romance.

One pen name. Three perspectives.

Three authors behind one continuing love story.

D.C.W. is the shared author identity of Daniel, Caleb, and Will, a real-life throuple who write the Tres Amigos, Una Vida series together.

The novels are fiction—not autobiography. Their relationship gives the work something different: an understanding of the tiny negotiations, private humor, difficult conversations, fierce loyalty, and daily tenderness that can exist when three people choose to build one life.

The series is published by 3Dudes1Life Creative and centers LGBTQ+ love, chosen family, belonging, and relationships that do not need to resemble tradition to be treated seriously.
