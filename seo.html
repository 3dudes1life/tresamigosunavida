Gay Throuple Romance Novel Inspired by a Real-Life Polyamorous Love Story

Tres Amigos, Una Vida is a powerful gay throuple romance novel that explores what happens when three men fall in love — not in theory, but in real life.

This MMF romance novel follows an emotionally guarded Navy mechanic from Oklahoma, a tattooed culinary specialist from Florida, and a sharp-tongued tech heir from San Francisco as they navigate vulnerability, jealousy, devotion, and the radical choice to build a life together.

An LGBTQ Polyamory Romance That Redefines Forever

Unlike traditional romance novels centered on two people, this LGBTQ throuple book explores polyamory in a committed, long-term relationship. It examines how love expands, how loyalty deepens, and how three hearts can build a shared home.

Readers searching for a gay Navy romance novel, queer poly relationship book, or unconventional love story will find a raw, emotional, and unforgettable journey inside these pages.

Why Readers Love This Gay MMF Romance Novel

  • Inspired by true events from a real-life throuple
  • Emotionally layered characters
  • Military and civilian life transitions
  • Messy, authentic polyamorous love
  • LGBTQ+ representation beyond stereotypes
