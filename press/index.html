Approved Short Bio

D.C.W.

D.C.W. is the shared pen name of Daniel, Caleb, and Will, a real-life throuple and the three authors behind the fictional Tres Amigos, Una Vida LGBTQ+ romance series. Their lived experience informs the emotional truth of stories centered on love, chosen family, belonging, and building a shared life outside conventional expectations. The series is published by 3Dudes1Life Creative.
